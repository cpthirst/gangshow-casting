## Backstage hotspot

Based on the cast parts and which item is up next, estimate how many people are likely to be in
each of three locations:

- **Backstage (Holding area)**: props, mics, quick change
- **Standby (Stage call)**: in the wings, waiting for their curtain call
- **On-stage**

Not all the cast will always be on, but we want to avoid crushes in the wings when the next
item's curtain call overlaps with the current one. The stage manager calls people to the
Backstage areas ~5–7 minutes before they're needed, and then ~3–5 minutes before curtain.

Using the roles cast, including ensemble headcount, show how many people will be in each of those
three locations, plotted as a graph or similar visualisation in the production menus.
